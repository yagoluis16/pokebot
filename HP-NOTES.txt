Technical note
The final HP target is re-read on every resolve/read call. During reallocation the pointer may temporarily be null or a memory read may fail; treat that as unavailable, not HP=0, and retry. Pointer resolution and scalar reading are not atomic, so a reallocation between them can still produce a failed or stale reading. Process memory region information is cached separately; newly allocated readable pages may require refreshing that information/re-attaching if address validation rejects them.
For stats at pointer + 4, this contract requires steps=[0], terminal=4, deref=false. With steps=[], terminal=4, deref=true the pointer is read at module + RVA + 4 instead.
